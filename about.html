Our Story

VZB Capital & Advisory was founded in 2021 in Belgrade with a clear mission: to bridge the gap between infrastructure development needs in the Western Balkans and the international capital required to realise them. Operating at the intersection of public policy, private finance, and infrastructure delivery, we bring together the expertise needed to navigate complex projects from conception through to financial close.

Our Mission

To accelerate sustainable infrastructure development across the Western Balkans by providing the highest quality strategic advisory to governments, developers, and investors.
